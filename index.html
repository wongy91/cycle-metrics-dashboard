Linear Cycle Metrics

rev 20260504 12:02PM (UTC+8)

Per-week, per-member success rate across multiple Linear teams (weekly + biweekly cycles). Data lives in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere except Linear's API.

Stored in browser localStorage only.

Notes: Per-Week Planned/Completed = ETL (1-week cycle) full pts + CD/EXL (2-week cycle) pts ÷ 2. Biweekly cycles are averaged across the 2 weeks they cover, so weekly totals don't double-count. Team-cycle Success Rate = Linear's cycle.progress (issue-count based, mirrors Linear's UI). Per-member success rates remain points-based since Linear doesn't report progress per assignee. Perf badge = mean of weekly per-member success rates (pts-based, each week weighted equally). High ≥80%, Mid 50–79%, Low <50%. Consistency badge = standard deviation of weekly success rates. Consistent σ<15, Variable σ=15–25, Inconsistent σ>25. Overall (in summary line) = total completed pts ÷ total planned pts — pts-weighted, so big weeks count more than small ones. Differs from Perf because Perf treats each week equally regardless of size.
